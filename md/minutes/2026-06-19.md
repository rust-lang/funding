### Review funding form applications

*Summary:* We summarized the applications received so far and discussed how to go about making decisions. We got a bit side-tracked into discussing whether or not we should have a 20% tax off the top up front or be more flexible and didn't make any decisions.

Discussion covered:

* We all agree we want to cover both contracting + maintenance. Some goals have maintenance costs for the work in question baked in. Others might put requests onto underfunded teams (e.g., Clippy).
* We have to be careful not to overpromise. We cannot be sure that we have multiple years of runway now. At the same time, our current sources of funding *at this time* will likely renew.
* About 40 applications. Many are from Compiler Team (biggest team, expected). Also have 3 team applications: Clippy, Rustdoc, Rustup. Three categories: bunch of people for MiR (employment/contracting mixed), people for project goals (2 groups: specific goal, want to work on whatever project goal can help with), work part time on something like a grant ($1-2k USD / month, with reviews, etc).

Prioritization approach:

* Break out MiR applicants first and judge against team needs.
* Then look at grants separately.
* Project Goals feel separate and more directed.
* Consider which teams/components are core vs. less core to the Rust Project.
* Think about how to partition funding so teams get at least partially funded.

Stability and contract structure:

* Foundation is not offering employment at this time (per RFC), but there's language on stable long-term funding.
* Current funding sources seem likely to renew at comparable levels, but we cannot promise this.
* Companies that fund have annual budgeting cycles — we should not make promises we might need to withdraw.
* Consensus: all contracts for now, until sustainable with more consistent funding contributions. We should emphasize to people that we expect to renew but cannot promise it and that we'll give them some amount of notice before the contract expires.

Maintenance "tax" discussion:

* If we get funding for project goals, should a percentage go toward maintenance of affected teams?
* Some existing goals already have 20% maintenance baked in, but others do not. Should we just put it into each goal on a case-by-case basis?
* Options discussed: explicit 20% line item, bake it into pitches, have it come off the top per-funder rather than per-goal.
* Concern that retroactively applying this could seem unfair to early funders who didn't have this requirement.
* No decision reached — will discuss further with those doing solicitation, potentially in t-funding-advisors or publicly.

Directed funding for specific individuals:

* Question raised about the case where a company or group of companies wants to fund a specific person or a person in a specific area. Should that be handled directly through Foundation or should it go through RFMF.
* Consensus: we would prefer for this to go through RFMF if it's going to be an RFMF-like arrangement, but we should talk more about the details. We left room for this in the RFC.
